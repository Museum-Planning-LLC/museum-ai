Western Kansas Weather Museum

A science museum concept for hands-on weather learning — developed with AI for science centers and communities starting a new museum.

Set inside the Rodeo and Agriculture Fairgrounds, the Western Kansas Weather Museum turns the region's dramatic weather into an interactive science experience. Visitors move through themed zones, each one built around a different side of the sky.

LocationRodeo and Agriculture Fairgrounds, Western Kansas
Building20,000 sq ft
Interactive exhibits10,000 sq ft
AudienceAges 8–12

The zones.

ZONE

Welcome Center

Staff greet visitors and guide them through the museum. Live weather screens and an oversized map show the climate patterns of Western Kansas.

ZONE

Lightning Show

Visitors watch lightning displays and learn the science behind them, then safely explore static electricity and how it changes with the weather.

ZONE

Climate Change Theater

An immersive presentation on how climate change affects the planet, with sustainable practices and everyday actions that help.

ZONE

Extreme Weather Simulator

Visitors step into a simulator pod for a virtual ride through a hurricane, blizzard, or heatwave, feeling the wind, rain, or snow while learning what causes them.

ZONE

Weather Art Studio

A creative space for sky-inspired painting, wind chimes, and weather-themed crafts to take home.

All renderings are AI-generated concepts.
