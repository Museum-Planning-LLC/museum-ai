About Museum AI.

A standalone site of Museum Planning LLC — museum consultants since 1999. AI-driven exhibit experiences and AI planning for science centers, natural history museums, local history museums, and communities starting a new museum.

Museum AI is a standalone site of Museum Planning LLC, for the firm’s work with artificial intelligence. We design exhibit experiences that use AI to engage visitors, personalize learning, and give smaller institutions capabilities that once required a large staff.

Our approach.

  • Strategic planning. We start with a plan for how AI fits your institution today, your future goals, and your mission, with visitor engagement and education at the center.
  • Phased integration. AI is introduced in stages so staff and visitors can adapt. Each phase builds on the one before it.
  • Visitor experience design. Interactive, informative exhibits for general audiences, with dedicated attention to family-oriented and more academic displays.
  • Community insight. We analyze visitor data and feedback to learn what drives engagement and learning, and use those findings to guide design.

The plans I produce are designed to be built, not filed. I have been on the other side of the table when a consultant delivers a master plan. I know what happens to that document when the meeting ends.
