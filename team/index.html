Managing Partner

Mark Walhimer

Mark Walhimer is the Managing Partner of Museum Planning LLC, which he founded in 1999. He has worked in museums since 1992 and has contributed to more than 40 museum projects across the United States, Mexico, and internationally, from science centers and children's museums to art, history, and natural history museums.

Before founding Museum Planning LLC he worked inside institutions, including the Children's Museum of Indianapolis, Liberty Science Center, and Discovery Science Center, and served as Chief Operating Officer of a museum exhibition design and fabrication firm.

He is the author of Museums 101 and Designing Museum Experiences, and of the 2026 white paper Convergence Era on industrial design, artificial intelligence, and cultural systems. He teaches industrial design.

He holds an M.S. in Industrial Design and Exhibition Design from Pratt Institute and a B.A. in Studio Art from Skidmore College.
