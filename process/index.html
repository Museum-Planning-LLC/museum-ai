Process

Our process.

Five stages for creating an interactive, immersive digital STEM experience, from first idea to measured results.

01

Ideation and Conceptualization

We clarify the client's goals and the key learning outcomes, then research the subject with experts and brainstorm ways to make complex STEM ideas engaging.

02

Design and Planning

We map the visitor journey with storyboards, build preliminary prototypes or simulations, and refine concepts with designers, technologists, and subject experts so they are educationally sound and technically feasible.

03

Development and Testing

We build the software and hardware interactives, then run repeated user testing and improve the experience based on what visitors tell us.

04

Implementation and Integration

We install the experience in its space, including displays and VR setups, and make sure it fits the physical and cultural context of the institution.

05

Launch and Evaluation

We open to the public, then track engagement, learning outcomes, and satisfaction to confirm the experience meets its goals.
