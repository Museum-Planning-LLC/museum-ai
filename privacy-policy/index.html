Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

This website, museum-ai.com, is operated by Museum Planning LLC. You can reach us at mark@museumplanning.com.

What we collect

Information you send us. When you use the contact form, it opens your own email program with your name, email address, and message filled in. We receive that information only if you choose to send the email. This website does not store form entries.

Usage information. If analytics are enabled on this site, the analytics provider may collect standard information such as pages visited, approximate location, browser type, and referring site. This information is used only to understand how the site is used.

Third-party content

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and images from our image host. Those services may receive your IP address when your browser requests the files. Links to social media and other websites are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

How we use your information

We use what you send us only to respond to your inquiry and to discuss potential projects. We do not sell or rent your information.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as it is useful for an ongoing conversation or project, and delete it on request.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing mark@museumplanning.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it last changed.
