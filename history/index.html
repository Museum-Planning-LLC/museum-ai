History

History

More than three decades in museums, and the move into artificial intelligence.

1992

Into museums

Mark Walhimer begins his museum career, working inside institutions including the Children's Museum of Indianapolis, Liberty Science Center, and Discovery Science Center.

1999

Museum Planning LLC founded

A personal practice covering the full arc of museum development, from feasibility through opening day.

2012–2013

Children's museums and science centers

Exhibition design for the Children's Museum of Manhattan and the Children's Museum of Indianapolis; master planning for Discovery Science Center.

2015

Museums 101

Mark's handbook on museum planning is published.

2016

Museo Memoria y Tolerancia

Exhibition design in Mexico City.

2019

A year of openings

MIDE (Museo Interactivo de Economía), The Tech Interactive, the Alcatraz: Life on the Rock traveling exhibition, and the C.O. Polk Interactive Museum in McDonough, Georgia.

2020

Discovery Center at Murfree Spring

Exhibition design in Murfreesboro, Tennessee.

2023

Museum AI launches

This site begins publishing AI-generated museum concepts: the LaGrange Children's Museum, the Western Kansas Weather Museum, Immersive Flowers, a diamond and mineral exhibition, and the Immersive AI Exhibition. Master planning for the Arizona Natural Resources Museum.

2024

A museum AI system

A proposal for a comprehensive museum AI platform. Master planning for the Howard Natural History Museum.

2026

Convergence Era

A white paper on industrial design, artificial intelligence, and cultural systems; Shared Ground, an embodied installation submitted to the Ars Electronica PRIX; and master planning for the Frehner Museum of Natural History.
