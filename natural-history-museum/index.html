Diamonds, Minerals & Jewelry · Three Concepts

Natural History Museum

AI-designed exhibition concepts for natural history museums — diamonds, minerals, and jewelry — from Museum Planning LLC.

CONCEPT 01

Bright and Airy

A clean, elegant gallery with a white palette, natural light, and minimal design.

  • Interactive displays on the history of diamonds, mining, and jewelry
  • Tablets and a smartphone app with in-depth information and interactives
  • A video wall of diamond and jewelry imagery
  • A touch table for virtual and augmented reality experiences
  • Artificial intelligence and blockchain to deepen interactivity
  • A kids' dress-up area and a jewelry giveaway
CONCEPT 02

Colorful, Playful, and Fun

An energetic space full of bold color, playful patterns, and whimsical details.

  • Diamond, mining, and jewelry exhibits with a playful, hands-on approach
  • Tablets and an app with gamified experiences and challenges
  • A video wall with dynamic, colorful visuals and sound
  • A touch table for virtual and augmented reality experiences
  • AI that offers personalized recommendations
  • A kids' area, a Makerspace, and live diamond-cutting and jewelry-making demonstrations
CONCEPT 03

Tropical Otherworld VR

A tropical world of lush foliage, saturated color, and immersive lighting.

  • VR journeys into diamond mines, ocean dives for gems, and tours of famous jewelry collections
  • VR headsets, with AI that adapts each journey to the visitor
  • A video wall of tropical landscapes and jewelry
  • A touch table for interactive exploration
  • Tablets and an app for more information and activities
  • A database of jewelry and diamond history, digital signage, and a theater

All renderings are AI-generated concepts.
